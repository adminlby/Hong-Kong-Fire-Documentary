2-alarm fire erupts at airport midfield concourse

發佈日期: 2026-02-10 20:06
TVB News
已複製連結
A 2-alarm fire broke out at the airport's Midfield Concourse.

Thick smoke was seen billowing from a plant room near Gate 212, prompting police to evacuate about 30 people. 

Firefighters brought the blaze mostly under control by 4 p.m.

Airport shuttle train services were temporarily disrupted, stranding some passengers.

No injuries were reported. 

The cause of the fire is under investigation.
